Subprocessors

Last updated 18 October 2026

These are the outside companies that handle personal data so ForgeCenter can work. Each one only gets what it needs for its job, and none of them may sell your data. This list is part of the privacy policy and the data processing agreement for business accounts.

Changes to this list

New subprocessors are added here at least 14 days before they start handling data. Business accounts are emailed when the list changes and can object by opening a support ticket.
