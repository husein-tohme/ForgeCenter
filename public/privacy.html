Privacy policy

Last updated 15 October 2026

What ForgeCenter collects

  • Your account basics from the service you sign in with (Google, Microsoft, GitHub or Discord): your name, verified email address, profile picture and account ID. ForgeCenter never sees your passwords.
  • Your phone number, if you sign in with WhatsApp.
  • Orders: what you bought, the price and whether payment succeeded. Stripe handles card details, so ForgeCenter never sees your card number.
  • What you write to us: project briefs, order messages, support tickets and showcase listings.
  • Your age group: teen or adult, and how and when an adult confirmed their age. ForgeCenter asks for your birthday once to check you're 13 or older, but doesn't store the date.
  • Your wallet: each top-up, payment, refund, reward and credit, so your balance is always on record.
  • Developer API: your API keys, stored only as a scrambled fingerprint that can't be turned back into the key, and a log of each call (which endpoint, how many AI tokens, what it cost).
  • Your creator page, if you make one: your handle, bio and links. It's public.
  • Referrals: which account referred yours, if you joined through someone's link, and the reward it earned them.
  • AI Maker assistants and Site Maker websites you create: their names, instructions, knowledge, descriptions and generated pages (your newest 20 sites).
  • Reports you send about content that breaks the rules, and any action taken on your account.
  • The names of things you make with the free tools while signed in: mods, texture packs and modpacks.
  • How many messages you send the Forge mentor each day, to apply the daily limit.

Servers and bots you buy

For Minecraft hosting, your name and email are used to create your account on the game server panel. For a Discord bot, the bot token you give us is stored encrypted and is only used to run your bot.

Confirming your age

Adults can confirm their age in one of two ways, both run by Stripe:

  • Credit card check. Stripe checks the card with your bank and tells ForgeCenter whether it's a credit card. You aren't charged, and the card is removed from your account straight after.
  • ID scan. Stripe Identity checks a photo ID and a selfie. ForgeCenter reads only your date of birth to work out whether you're 18 or over, doesn't store it, and then asks Stripe to delete the images and details from the check.

Either way, ForgeCenter keeps only the result: adult or not, the method and the date.

Support tickets

Forge Support, an AI assistant run by Anthropic, reads your ticket together with your name, age group, wallet balance and the status of your orders, servers and bots, so it can answer. It doesn't see other customers' details, payment card details or passwords. Tickets are kept until you delete your account.

What's public

Your creator page shows your display name, profile picture, handle, bio, links, badges, the mods you published to Modrinth and your approved showcase listings. It never shows your email, phone number, age group, wallet or orders. Showcase listings show your display name.

What ForgeCenter doesn't keep

  • Your chats with AI Maker assistants. They're sent to Anthropic to write the answers and aren't saved by ForgeCenter.
  • The full text of your API keys. Copy a key when it's created; it can't be shown again.
  • Your date of birth, card details or ID images.
  • Your conversations with Forge. They're sent to Anthropic to write the answers and aren't saved by ForgeCenter.
  • Your Modrinth token. It's used once to create your draft and then discarded.

Cookies

ForgeCenter sets one cookie to keep you signed in, and a short-lived one while you sign in with Microsoft, GitHub or Discord. If someone under 13 tries to make an account, a cookie stops that browser from trying again for a day. There are no advertising or tracking cookies. Google may set its own cookies when you use its sign-in button.

Who your data is shared with

  • Google, Microsoft, GitHub or Discord, whichever you choose to sign in with.
  • Meta (WhatsApp), to send your sign-in code if you sign in with your phone number.
  • Stripe, to take payments, manage subscriptions and wallet top-ups, and run age checks.
  • Anthropic, to answer Forge mentor questions, support tickets, AI Maker chats, Site Maker requests and AI calls made through the developer API.
  • Resend, to send you emails about your orders.
  • Modrinth, when you search for mods or choose to publish a mod there.
  • The company that hosts this website, which stores the database.

Your data is never sold.

Deleting your data

Delete your account any time from My account. That removes your profile, creator page, API keys, assistants, saved sites and creations, showcase listings, messages, support tickets and age checks, and switches off your game server panel login. Records of past payments and wallet activity are kept for tax law, without your name attached.

Age

ForgeCenter is for people 13 and older. If someone says they're under 13, their new account is deleted straight away. Every account starts in the teen group; some purchases, marked in the prices, and 18+ showcase listings are only for confirmed adults. If you're under 18, ask a parent or guardian before buying anything.

Contact

Questions about your data? Email us using the address on your Stripe receipt.
