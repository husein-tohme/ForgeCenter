Questions

    
Who owns what ForgeCenter makes?
You do. Mods are published from your Modrinth account, payments go to your Stripe account, and websites use your domain. We never publish anything without your approval.

    
Do I need my own Stripe account?
Only if your project takes payments. You connect it once and money from your customers goes straight to you.

    
How much does a custom game cost?
$499 to $2,000 depending on size. The $499 deposit starts the project, and the final price is agreed in writing before work goes past it.

    
What do I get with Minecraft hosting?
For $8 a month, a server for 10 players with 2 GB of RAM, 100 GB of storage, a dedicated CPU and DDoS protection, billed monthly. Cancel any time.

    
How do I sign in?
With an account you already have: Google (including Gmail), Microsoft (Outlook, Hotmail or Xbox), GitHub, Discord, or your phone number through WhatsApp. That way every account belongs to a real person. ForgeCenter never sees your password, and you can connect more than one so you never get locked out.

    
What are orbs?
ForgeCenter's currency: 1 orb is 1 cent. Buy orbs by card and spend on anything with a one-time price, like a Discord bot or a showcase slot, in one step. It holds up to 200,000 orbs ($2,000), doesn't expire, and can't be withdrawn or sent to someone else. Find it on your account page.

    
Why is my account in the teen group?
Like on Discord, every account starts in the teen group. Adults confirm their age once, with a credit card check or an ID scan and selfie through Stripe, to unlock adult-only purchases (marked in the prices) and 18+ showcase listings. ForgeCenter keeps only the result. Accounts are for people 13 and older.

    
How do I get help?
Open a ticket on the help page. Forge Support, an AI assistant, answers straight away and passes anything it can't solve, such as refunds, to a person.

    
Is the AI assistant really mine?
It's branded and set up for you, with its own instructions and higher limits. It runs on a leading AI model behind the scenes, so it stays fast and up to date.
