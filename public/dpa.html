Data processing agreement

Last updated 18 October 2026 · For business accounts

This agreement applies when a business ("you") puts other people's personal data into ForgeCenter, for example the members of a Discord server your bot runs in, or visitors to a site you host here. For that data you are the controller and ForgeCenter is your processor. It's part of the terms of service, so you don't need to sign anything separately.

What ForgeCenter does

  • Processes the data only to run the services you ordered, and only on your instructions (your settings and requests count as instructions).
  • Keeps it confidential: only people who need it to run or support the service can see it, and they're bound to confidentiality.
  • Protects it with encryption in transit (HTTPS), encrypted secrets and backups, access controls and logging.
  • Uses only the listed subprocessors, under contracts with the same level of protection, and tells you 14 days before adding one.
  • Helps you answer people who ask to see, fix or delete their data.
  • Tells you without undue delay, and within 72 hours of finding out, if a breach affects your data.
  • Deletes or returns the data when you end the service (download it first from My account), except where the law requires keeping records.

Transfers outside the EU and UK

Some subprocessors are in the USA. Those transfers rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

What you do

  • Make sure you're allowed to collect the data and have told the people it's about.
  • Don't put special-category data (health, religion and so on) or children's data into a service unless the law allows it and you've told ForgeCenter.

Audits and questions

Open a support ticket on the Help page to ask about security, request this agreement as a signed PDF, or ask for audit information.
