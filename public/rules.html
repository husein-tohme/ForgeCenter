ForgeCenter rules

Everyone who uses ForgeCenter agrees to these rules. They keep it a good place to build, play and learn, for teenagers and adults alike.

The short version

  • Be yourself: one real person, 13 or older, per account.
  • Only share what you made, or have permission to share.
  • No cheats, malware, scams, hate or harassment.
  • Wallet credit is for ForgeCenter only. Never sell or trade it.

Who can use ForgeCenter

  • You must be 13 or older to have an account.
  • One person per account. Don't share your account, and don't make extra accounts to get around a limit or a suspension.
  • Sign in with your own real account from Google, Microsoft, GitHub, Discord or WhatsApp. Don't use someone else's.

What's not allowed

Anywhere on ForgeCenter, including showcase listings, support tickets, order messages, bots, servers and what you make with the free tools:

  • Cheats and hacked clients. No cheat clients, exploits, duping tools or anything made to break other people's games or servers.
  • Malware. No viruses, token grabbers, account stealers or downloads that do something different from what they say.
  • Stolen or reuploaded work. Don't post mods, maps, textures, plugins or code made by someone else as if they were yours.
  • Other people's personal info. Don't share anyone's real name, address, phone number, photos or accounts without their permission.
  • Harassment and hate. No bullying, threats, or attacks on people for who they are.
  • Scams and phishing. No fake giveaways, fake login pages, or tricking people into handing over money, passwords or items.
  • Impersonation. Don't pretend to be another person, a creator, a company, or ForgeCenter staff.
  • Sexual content. None at all. Anything else meant for adults only belongs in listings marked 18+, which only confirmed adults can see.
  • Someone else's card or ID for age checks. Only confirm your age with your own card or your own ID.
  • Selling or trading wallet credit for real money. Or for items, accounts or anything else outside ForgeCenter.
  • Spamming the AI or support. Don't flood Forge, the AI tools or the help desk with messages, or try to trick them into breaking these rules.

Rules for creators

  • Only post what you made yourself, or what you have the rights to share. Give credit when you use other people's free assets.
  • Minecraft content must follow Mojang's EULA and usage guidelines. Roblox content must follow Roblox's terms.
  • Support and donation links must be honest: say what the money is for, and only link to your own pages.
  • Showcase links must go where they say they go.

Rules for the AI tools

  • Don't use Forge, Forge Support or the other AI tools to make anything harmful, like malware, scams or content that breaks these rules.
  • AI can be wrong. Check important code and advice before you rely on it.
  • Forge Support can't give refunds, credit or discounts. Only a person at ForgeCenter can.

Wallet rules

  • Wallet credit is store credit for ForgeCenter. It can't be cashed out.
  • Credit can't be sent to another account.
  • If a top-up is refunded or charged back, that credit is removed from your wallet.

When rules are broken

Anyone can report a showcase listing with the Report button on it. A person at ForgeCenter reads every report. Depending on what happened, we may:

  • send a warning,
  • remove the content,
  • suspend the account, so it can't buy, post or use signed-in tools until the suspension ends,
  • or ban the account for good, for serious or repeated problems like malware, scams or using someone else's card.

Paid orders on a banned account may be cancelled. Anything illegal may be reported to the police.

How to appeal

If you think we got it wrong, open a ticket on the Help page and choose "Account, sign-in or age check". Suspended accounts can still sign in and open tickets. Tell us what happened, and a person will look at it again.

These rules sit alongside the terms of service and the privacy policy.
