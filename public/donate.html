    Good to know

    Is my donation tax-deductible?

    No. ForgeCenter is a small business, not a charity, so donations are a gift to the business and can't be deducted from your taxes.

    Do I get anything for donating?

    A warm thank you, and your name on the supporters wall if you're signed in and choose to show it. Donations don't unlock anything, and everything free stays free either way.

    Can I get a refund?

    Donations aren't refunded, except for mistakes such as a double charge or a wrong amount. If that happens, open a ticket on the help page and it'll be put right.

    How do I stop a monthly donation?

    Open your account and choose Manage billing. Cancelling stops future payments; months you've already given for aren't refunded.
